📄 Get Hired

Resume, Portfolio & Interview

Practical tips to land your first job faster

📄

Resume Building

  • Keep it to 1 page — recruiters spend 6 seconds
  • Use ATS-friendly templates (no tables/columns)
  • Quantify achievements: "Increased speed by 40%"
  • Use action verbs: Built, Designed, Improved
  • Tailor resume for every job posting
🐙

GitHub Profile

  • Pin your 4–6 best projects
  • Write clear README for every project
  • Maintain a green contribution graph
  • Deploy projects live (Vercel/Netlify)
  • Contribute to open source
💼

LinkedIn Profile

  • Professional headshot (increases views 14x)
  • Write a strong headline with keywords
  • Post content weekly to grow visibility
  • Connect with 500+ people in your field
  • Turn on "Open to Work" privately
🎤

Interview Prep

  • Practice 50 DSA problems on LeetCode
  • Use STAR method for behavioral questions
  • Research the company deeply before interview
  • Prepare 3 strong project walkthroughs
  • Do 5+ mock interviews with friends
🏢

Internship Prep

  • Apply early — start 3 months before semester
  • Use Internshala, LinkedIn, AngelList
  • Cold email founders at startups (it works!)
  • Have a 60-second elevator pitch ready
  • Follow up within 48 hours after interview
🌐

Portfolio Website

  • Build a personal website — it sets you apart
  • Include About, Projects, Skills, Contact
  • Show live demos, not just screenshots
  • Get a custom domain (₹700/year)
  • Add a testimonials section if possible
